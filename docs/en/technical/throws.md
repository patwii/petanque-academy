# Palette of Throws

## The Complete Technical Repertoire

This is an overview of the throws available in pétanque. You don't need to master all of them, but knowing what exists helps you understand the full scope of the game.

## Pointing Techniques

### By Trajectory
- **Roulette** (rolling) - Low trajectory, rolls most of the way
- **Demi-portée** (half-lob) - Medium trajectory, lands halfway
- **Portée** (lob) - High trajectory, lands near target
- **Plombée** (drop shot) - Very high, drops almost vertically

### By Spin
- **Full spin (backspin)** - Maximum backspin, stops or pulls back on landing
- **Half spin** - Moderate backspin, controlled roll after landing
- **No spin** - Neutral, natural roll on landing

## Shooting Techniques

### By Impact Point
- **Au fer** (iron shot) - Direct hit on the boule
- **Devant** (in front) - Landing just before the target
- **Sautée** (jump shot) - Bouncing into the target

### By Intensity
- **Hard flat shot** - Direct, powerful hit with flat trajectory
- **Medium hard** - Balanced power and control
- **Soft with high arch** - Lob shot, drops onto target from above

## Mastery Path

Remember: The goal isn't to master everything. It's to have enough technical foundation that you can enter the zone and let your body choose the right throw naturally.
