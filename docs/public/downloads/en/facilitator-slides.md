## Slide 5: Your Brain Has Two Modes 🧠

### 🤔 PLANNING MODE (Analytical Brain)

**When to use:** BEFORE the shot
- 📊 Analyzing the terrain
- 🎯 Choosing your strategy
- ⚖️ Weighing the risks
- 🤝 Deciding the shot

### 🎯 EXECUTION MODE (Flow Brain)

**When to use:** DURING the shot
- 💪 Trusting your body
- 🚫 NOT thinking about technique
- ⏰ Present moment only
- 🤖 Automatic performance

### ⚠️ THE PROBLEM:
**Most players stay in Planning Mode during execution!**
*Your analytical brain interferes with your automatic performance.*

## Slide 6: The Switch - Your Secret Weapon 🔄

### Master This One Skill:

```
BEFORE the shot:          DURING the shot:
🤔 Planning Mode    →     🎯 Execution Mode
```

### The Process:

**1️⃣ BEFORE (10-15 seconds):**
- 🤔 Analyze everything
- 📊 Plan your strategy
- ✅ Make your decision

**2️⃣ THE SWITCH (3 breaths):**
- 🌬️ Breathe deeply
- 🧘 Clear your mind
- 🔄 Let go of analysis

**3️⃣ DURING (2-3 seconds):**
- 🎯 Trust completely
- 🤖 Let it happen
- ⚡ Execute automatically

**Practice this switch = Access flow on demand!**

## Slide 11: Tool #1 - The 3-Breath Reset 🌬️

### Your Emergency Pressure Release Valve

**Use this when:**
- 😤 You just made a mistake
- 😰 You feel pressure building
- 🤯 Your mind is racing

### The Process:

**🌬️ BREATH 1 (Inhale 4 sec):**
Notice where you feel tension (shoulders? jaw? stomach?)

**🌬️ BREATH 2 (Exhale 6 sec):**
Release that tension. Let it flow out with your breath.

**🌬️ BREATH 3 (Inhale 4, Exhale 6):**
Refocus on NOW. This shot. This moment. Nothing else.

**⏱️ Takes 20 seconds. Changes everything.**

## Slide 13: Tool #2 - Your Pre-Shot Routine 🎯

### Build Automatic Consistency

**The 4-Step Routine:**

**1️⃣ VISUALIZE (3 seconds)**
See the perfect shot in your mind. Watch it land exactly where you want.

**2️⃣ BREATHE (2 seconds)**
One deep breath. Switch from planning to execution mode.

**3️⃣ ANCHOR (2 seconds)**
Touch your boule. Feel the weight. Physical connection.

**4️⃣ EXECUTE (2 seconds)**
Trust completely. Let your body do what it knows.

**⏱️ Total: 9 seconds. Same every time.**

### Why It Works:
**Consistency = Confidence = Performance**

---

## Slide 14: Tool #3 - Mistake Recovery Protocol 🔄

### Bad Shot? Here's Your 4-Step Recovery:

**1️⃣ ACKNOWLEDGE (2 seconds)**
"That didn't go as planned." (No judgment, just fact)

**2️⃣ RELEASE (3 seconds)**
Physical reset: Shake your hands, roll your shoulders, walk 3 steps.

**3️⃣ REFOCUS (3 seconds)**
"Next shot is a NEW opportunity." (Not connected to the last one)

**4️⃣ EXECUTE (Use your routine)**
Trust your pre-shot routine. Fresh start.

### The Key Insight:
**Each shot is independent. Don't carry mistakes forward.**

*The best players have the shortest memory for bad shots.*
