# Mental Game Introduction - Participant Handout

## Welcome

Today you'll learn practical mental game techniques to improve your pétanque performance.

### Session Objectives
- Understand flow states and how to access them
- Recognize and manage your inner critic
- Learn practical pressure management tools
- Create a simple pre-shot routine

### Ground Rules
1. What's shared here stays here
2. No judgment of others' experiences
3. Participation is voluntary
4. There are no wrong answers

---

## The Zone / Flow State

### What Is Flow?
Flow is when you're completely absorbed in the game. Time seems different, 
movements feel automatic, and performance is effortless.

### Two Modes of Playing

**Planning Mode (Outside Circle)**
- Analyze the situation
- Choose your strategy
- Decide throw type
- Think about technique

**Execution Mode (Inside Circle)**
- Eyes on target only
- Trust your training
- Automatic movement
- No analysis

### The Switch
The moment you step into the circle is your "switch" from thinking to doing.

**Practice:** As you step in, take one breath and let go of all analysis.

---

## The Inner Critic

### Two Inner Voices

**Inner Critic (Unhelpful)**
- "How could you miss that?!"
- "You always choke under pressure"
- "You're terrible at this"
- Harsh, judgmental, absolute

**Inner Coach (Helpful)**
- "That went left"
- "Let's try again"
- "What can I learn?"
- Neutral, factual, growth-oriented

### Your Inner Critic Patterns

Write down what your inner voice says:

_________________________________________________

Under pressure:
_________________________________________________

_________________________________________________

### Reframing Practice

Choose one Inner Critic statement. How would an Inner Coach say it?

Inner Critic: _________________________________________________

Inner Coach: _________________________________________________

---

## Practical Tools

### Tool 1: 3-Breath Reset

Use this when you feel pressure building or after a mistake.

1. Breathe in for 4 counts
2. Hold for 2 counts
3. Breathe out for 6 counts
4. Repeat 3 times

**When to use:** Before important throws, after mistakes, when feeling tense

---

### Tool 2: Simple Pre-Shot Routine

**Outside Circle (Planning - 10 seconds)**
1. Look at the situation
2. Decide on your throw
3. Visualize the result

**Walking to Circle (Transition - 3 seconds)**
1. One deep breath
2. Let go of analysis

**Inside Circle (Execution - 5 seconds)**
1. Eyes on target only
2. Trust your training
3. Execute

**Your Routine:**

Outside Circle: _________________________________________________

Transition: _________________________________________________

Inside Circle: _________________________________________________

---

### Tool 3: Mistake Recovery Protocol

Elite players don't make fewer mistakes - they recover faster.

**3 Steps:**

1. **Observe** - State the fact neutrally
   - Not: "I'm so stupid!"
   - But: "That went left"

2. **Learn** - Extract useful information
   - "Wind stronger than I thought"
   - "Released too early"

3. **Reset** - Move forward
   - Use 3-breath reset
   - Eyes forward, not backward

# Mental Game Quick Reference

## The 3 Core Concepts

### 1. The Zone (Flow State)
**What:** Complete absorption in the game
**How:** Switch from planning to execution
**Key:** Let go of thinking when you step into the circle

### 2. The Inner Critic
**What:** Harsh, judgmental self-talk
**How:** Reframe to Inner Coach voice
**Key:** Neutral observation, not harsh judgment

### 3. Pressure Response
**What:** Physical and mental tension under pressure
**How:** Use reset techniques
**Key:** Perform well despite nerves, not without them

---

## The 3 Essential Tools

### Tool 1: 3-Breath Reset
1. In for 4, hold for 2, out for 6
2. Repeat 3 times
3. Use before important throws or after mistakes

### Tool 2: Pre-Shot Routine
- **Outside:** Look, decide, visualize (10 sec)
- **Transition:** Breathe, let go (3 sec)
- **Inside:** Eyes on target, execute (5 sec)

### Tool 3: Mistake Recovery
1. **Observe:** "That went left" (neutral fact)
2. **Learn:** "Wind stronger" (extract info)
3. **Reset:** 3-breath reset, eyes forward

---

## This Week's Practice

□ Practice 3-breath reset daily
□ Create your pre-shot routine
□ Notice your Inner Critic patterns
□ Use mistake recovery protocol

## Hur man använder dessa material

### Före sessionen
1. **Granska allt material** minst 1 vecka innan
2. **Bokmärk viktiga sidor** för enkel åtkomst under sessionen
3. **Öva på din presentation** med hjälp av handledarens slides disposition
4. **Testa skärmdelning** om du presenterar på distans

### Under sessionen
1. **Dela din skärm** eller använd en projektor för att visa innehåll
2. **Dela sidlänkar** med deltagare via chatt eller QR-kod
3. **Vägled deltagarna** genom övningarna med hjälp av arbetsbladen
4. **Referensmaterial** när du faciliterar

### Efter sessionen
1. **Dela alla sidlänkar** med deltagarna via e-post
2. **Skicka länkar inom 24 timmar** för enkel referens
3. **Uppmuntra digital bokmärkning** för fortsatt lärande
