## Exercise 6: Mode Switching Practice

### Planning vs Execution

**Practice the switch:**

**Planning Phase (Before shot):**
- What do I need to analyze?
- What's my strategy?
- What shot will I choose?

**Execution Phase (During shot):**
- Clear your mind
- Trust your body
- Let it happen

**Practice Exercise:**

Take 5 practice shots. For each:

1. Spend 10 seconds in planning mode (analyze, decide)
2. Take 3 breaths to switch modes
3. Execute in flow mode (no thinking)

**Reflection:**
- Could you feel the difference between modes?
- Was the switch clear?
- Which mode is harder for you?
