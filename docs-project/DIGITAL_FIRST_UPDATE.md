### Environmental
- ✅ No paper waste
- ✅ No printing costs
- ✅ Reduced carbon footprint

### Practical
- ✅ Always up-to-date content
- ✅ Accessible on any device
- ✅ Easy to share via links
- ✅ No printing logistics
- ✅ Searchable content
- ✅ Interactive links work

- ✅ Share with teammates easily
- ✅ No lost or damaged papers
- ✅ Multi-device sync

## 📱 New Sharing Methods

### QR Codes
Generate QR codes for easy in-person sharing:
- Point phones at screen
- Instant access to materials
- No typing URLs

### Link Sharing
Share via:
- Email
- Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.)
- Social media
- Club websites
- Video conferencing chat

### Screen Sharing
For remote/hybrid sessions:
- Share browser window
- Participants follow along on own devices
- Interactive and engaging
