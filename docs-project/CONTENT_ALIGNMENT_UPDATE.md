## 📊 Consistent Structure Across All Pages

### Standard Quick Access Format

All pages now follow this pattern:

```markdown
## Quick Access

| Section/Template | Purpose | Access |
|------------------|---------|--------|
| **Item 1** | Description | [View X](#link) |
| **Item 2** | Description | [View Y](#link) |
| **Item 3** | Description | [View Z](#link) |
| **Related** | Other resources | [Link 1](/path) • [Link 2](/path) |
```

### Benefits

**For Users:**
- ✅ Quick navigation to specific sections
- ✅ Clear overview of page content
- ✅ Easy access to related resources
- ✅ Consistent experience across all pages

**For Site:**

## 🎯 Content Alignment Principles

### 1. Digital-First Language

**Before:**
- "Copy the sections below into your preferred format (Word, Google Docs, Excel, Google Sheets, or a notebook)"
- "Print handouts"
- "Download PDF"

**After:**
- "Use the templates below directly on this page, or copy them to your preferred digital tool"
- "Bookmark materials pages"
- "View Guide" / "View Template"

### 2. Consistent Navigation

**All pages now have:**
- Quick Access table at the top
- Clear section links
- Related resources links
- Digital-first tips where appropriate

### 3. Cross-Linking

**Every guide page links to related guides:**
- Mental Journey ↔ Workshop ↔ Training Camp ↔ Training Session
- Templates linked from guides
- Materials linked from session guides
